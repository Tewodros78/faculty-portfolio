Tewodros G. Gutema

PhD Candidate in Economics · Howard University · Washington, DC

Development Economics Labor Economics Applied Microeconometrics Health Economics

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Howard University, specializing in Development and Labor Economics. I am currently a Research Fellow at the Center for Equitable Economy and Sustainable Society (e2s2) at Howard University. I have worked as a lecturer and researcher at a public university for six years. I also have experience in the banking sector as a senior research and strategy officer. As an applied microeconomist, I focus on Development, Labor, and Health Economics, with an emphasis on interactions among education, income, and wealth inequalities in marginalized societies, as well as on the quantitative analysis of social welfare distribution and poverty reduction.

Career Goals

My goal is to join a research-active economics department as a tenure-track faculty member, where I can build a research program on conflict, climate, and food security in developing economies while teaching econometrics, development economics, and applied data analysis. I want to train students, especially first-generation students and students from underrepresented backgrounds, to use data and rigorous methods to answer policy questions that matter to their communities. I also aim to work with development and humanitarian institutions so that my research informs how shocks are anticipated and how aid is targeted.
