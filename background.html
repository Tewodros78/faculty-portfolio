Professional Biography

Tewodros G. Gutema is a PhD candidate in Economics at Howard University and a Research Fellow at the Center for Equitable Economy and Sustainable Society (E2S2). His research is in applied microeconometrics and development economics, with a focus on how conflict, climate shocks, and price shocks affect food insecurity and household welfare in Ethiopia. His dissertation uses a dynamic difference-in-differences design to estimate the effect of conflict escalation on acute food insecurity across Ethiopian regions from 2010 to 2023.

Before joining Howard, Tewodros taught undergraduate economics for more than six years as a Lecturer and Researcher at Salale University, where he also advised senior essays and theses and led community research on rural livelihood diversification. He has worked as Senior Strategy Officer at Amhara Bank and as head of the Project Finance and Economic Department in the Addis Ababa City Government Administration. His work has been published in Cogent Economics & Finance and presented at the Econometric Society African Meeting, the GLO Job Market Session, and the Southern Economic Association Annual Meeting. He was selected for the Russell Sage Foundation Summer Institute in Behavioral Economics in 2026.
